Build and run a city. Zone land for homes, shops and industry, connect it with roads,
rail and power, balance the budget, and keep your citizens happy — while fires, floods,
tornadoes and the occasional monster try to spoil your plans.

<b>Andropolis</b> is an open-source Android port of <b>Micropolis</b>, the GPL release of the
original SimCity city simulator by Will Wright. It uses the MicropolisCore engine: the
original simulation code, in C++, unchanged.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>The full classic simulation: zones, traffic, pollution, crime, land value, budget, evaluation</li>
<li>The 8 original scenarios (Dullsville, San Francisco, Hamburg, Bern, Tokyo, Detroit, Boston, Rio) and 23 sample cities</li>
<li>New maps with terrain choices: trees, lakes, rivers, islands</li>
<li>Touch controls: drag roads and power lines, place buildings with a preview, per-build undo</li>
<li>Overlays, graphs, messages, the citizens' opinion poll and the annual report</li>
<li>Optional background play: the city keeps going while the app is closed, at a pace you choose, and notifies you when something happens</li>
<li>Saves are plain files you can copy and share</li>
<li>No ads, no tracking, no network access</li>
</ul>
